Method

How the matches were run

Each match pitted the real browser engine against the other program, with colours alternated and varied openings. After every move the complete board was compared between both programs, so differences in how they read the rules could not skew a result. The details are in the full technical report.

Fine print: no games against human experts yet · GF1 was not tested beyond depth 6 · standard GIPF rules.
