# Baseline report status

The canonical fixed-baseline reports are the unversioned `baseline-*.json`
files in this directory. They were produced with baseline
`v1.2-paired-seeds`: fixed heuristic weights, seeded equal-value tie breaking,
and PRNG streams reset per colour-balanced opening pair.

Earlier baseline reports and their JSONL heartbeats are retained under
`archive/`. They predate paired seeds and are not valid comparison references.
